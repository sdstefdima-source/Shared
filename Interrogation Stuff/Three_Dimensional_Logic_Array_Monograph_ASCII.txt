Orthogonal cases, typed interactions, and certified continuation
----------------------------------------------------------------

A construction specification for extending linked logic-puzzle matrices into a
spatially organized verification workbench. The array contains propositions,
relations, models, witnesses, and proof records. Its geometry organizes these
objects; its inference rules determine what follows.

The central result is constructive. Given a nonempty finite semantic domain
and finitely many interpreted propositions, repeated Boolean splitting
produces a disjoint, exhaustive partition. Every original proposition is
recovered as a union of its partition cells. Stable refinement preserves this
representation as further propositions are introduced. A separate
transition-preservation condition makes the same representation suitable for
modal questions.

Logical layer:     a AND b = FALSE  means disjoint cases.
Display layer:     layout(record) = (x,y,z) in ordinary 3D.
Verification:      premises + negated claim -> checked contradiction.

These are different operations. An arbitrarily large family of cases can be
logically orthogonal. More than three nonzero vectors cannot all be mutually
perpendicular in three-dimensional Euclidean space. The design therefore uses
a 3D atlas of a potentially much larger semantic algebra, not a claim that
spatial rotation solves logic.

The retained spatial convention is center = shared theory; south/negative x =
cases; north/positive x = diagnostics; negative y = witnesses and choice
dependencies; positive y = provenance; positive/negative z = the two sides of
a declared classical query. The signed CH and choice example remains the
principal continuity case study [P1].

Deliverable and claim boundary
------------------------------

This volume supplies definitions, proof obligations, constructive algorithms,
worked examples, counterexamples, and a backend architecture. Its companion
verifier executes finite Boolean, modal, temporal, and evidence tests. It does
not implement an unrestricted higher-order decision procedure or certify the
imported infinite set-theoretic theorems. The exact distinction between
representation, proof checking, and automatic decision is part of the design
[R10-R12].

Notation used in both editions
------------------------------

Gamma      the declared hard premises of a context
Omega      admissible complete interpretations under those premises
E          interpretations consistent with current evidence
[[phi]]    truth region of formula phi in the specified model family
A_n        a finite partition into nonempty observable cases
rho        a restriction or parent map, with its domain declared
R_a        accessibility relation for action or modality a
Core(W)    formulas true throughout the nonempty family W
TRUE/FALSE top and bottom of a classical Boolean algebra

AND, OR, NOT, ->, and <-> denote classical conjunction, disjunction, negation,
implication, and equivalence unless an adapter explicitly declares different
semantics. Braces describe sets; brackets after a modality indicate its
indexed relation. A support bit in the later four-valued adapter is evidence
metadata, not another use of classical truth.

A proposition is atomic syntactically when its internal structure is not
expanded by the current language. A Boolean atom is a minimal nonempty
distinguishable case in a particular finite algebra. Neither is a physical
point. A context is an evaluation scope; a displayed cube is a container for
records; a possible world is a semantic object. These terms are never
interchangeable.

Evidence labels
---------------

[P] identifies inspected predecessor material. [R] identifies an external
primary paper or official specification. A displayed proposition proved in
this volume is a derivation under its stated assumptions. A test result
establishes only its finite declared range. A proposed extension is labeled as
an interface or design obligation, not an already implemented solver.
Numerical subscripts index records and stages; they do not convert the
proposition grid into an arithmetic puzzle.

The inspected starting point
----------------------------

The immediate basis is CH_Choice_Cubical_Logic_Report.txt and the JavaScript
kernel shipped with its verification archive. The earlier signed-layer checker
and the supplied staircase image were also inspected. The image is the visual
precedent for connected pairwise relation blocks, not a source of logical
axioms. File hashes and inspected paths accompany the reproducibility package
[P1-P3].

The predecessor has a small explicit semantic universe: alpha, beta, gamma.
Its current grammar accepts H, C, AC, truth constants, Boolean connectives,
and unary box/diamond operators. It calculates truth regions in an explicit
frame, then intersects current-world evidence. It also separates witness
selection from evidence mutation and labels its extra refinement tags as a
propositional sandbox rather than newly discovered set-theoretic sentences
[P1, sections 4-8; P2].

These commitments are retained. The present volume generalizes the data model
and mathematical interfaces; it does not retroactively claim that the
predecessor already handles arbitrary quantifiers, temporal logics,
paraconsistency, or a general 3D runtime. The new verifier checks specified
finite mechanisms. The blueprint for other backends is distinct from
implemented coverage.

Theorem inputs versus derived consequences
------------------------------------------

The CH example imports the constructibility implication V=L -> CH, the
relevant Cohen iteration behavior, and preservation of ZFC in its permitted
extensions. It uses those inputs to derive finite modal consequences. AC is a
premise shared by these worlds, not an empirical conclusion generated by a
negative-y position [R6-R8].

current-answer audit, or newly verified attribution to that answer is claimed.
The continuing example is grounded in the actual predecessor specification and
the independently consulted primary forcing papers. The distinction prevents a
remembered discussion from acquiring the status of a freshly inspected proof.

New construction supplied here
------------------------------

The extension is a typed atlas of constraint relations and case partitions.
New material includes complete interaction storage, a general atomization
theorem, a proof that layout changes preserve no extra truth, executable modal
partition refinement, finite temporal fixed points, and an adapter contract
for quantified and nonclassical languages. The prose preserves the inherited
north/south and signed-layer terminology while making its limits formal.

The strongest justified general claim is conditional: every admitted adapter
must preserve its source semantics and expose a checker for its certificates.
Composing exact interfaces preserves a checked conclusion. It does not imply
that every input will be decided, that every useful representation is small,
or that consistent premises describe reality [R10-R12].

The predecessor kernel was rerun rather than merely citing its historical
totals. Its results are reported separately from the new checks on page 36.
Browser screenshots inside the old archive are historical evidence, not a new
browser test performed for this monograph.

THE TYPED LOGICAL OBJECT
==============================================================================

Start with meaning, not coordinates
-----------------------------------

A workbench instance must name its logic, signature, hard theory, context
family, observation contract, and requested query. The signature lists
predicate names, argument types, constants, variables, and operators. Scope
records distinguish the same predicate evaluated at different worlds, times,
agents, or objects. A bare string is insufficient as a logical identity.

Instance = (logic_id, signature, Gamma, contexts, relations,
            evidence, query, requested_guarantee, revision).
Formula  = a typed syntax tree with explicit binders and scope.
Record   = (id, kind, formula_id, context_id, source_id,
            proof_dependencies, status, revision).
Layout   = a separate map from record IDs to display coordinates.

For example, DoorClosed@world-w and DoorClosed@world-v share a predicate
symbol but need not have the same value. An alias to the first record in
another grid is the same evaluation occurrence. A new world-scoped occurrence
is not an alias. These distinctions prevent cross-panel copies from
manufacturing corroboration or a change of context from manufacturing a
contradiction.

Exact finite semantics
----------------------

For a finite propositional signature P, a complete interpretation is a map
v:P->{false,true}. Let Omega be the set of interpretations satisfying Gamma.
The report assumes Omega is nonempty whenever it constructs an ordinary
Boolean algebra of surviving cases. The empty case is reported separately.

Omega = {v : v satisfies Gamma}.
[[phi]] = {v in Omega : v satisfies phi}.
Gamma entails phi  iff  [[phi]] = Omega.

The definition supplies a semantic target, not necessarily a cheap enumeration
procedure. For finite models of other logics, Omega may instead contain
structures, pointed transition systems, or valuations of ground instances.
Every change of meaning requires an adapter; the same notation must not
silently switch from a set of assignments to a set of epistemic alternatives.

Three independently checkable interfaces
----------------------------------------

A syntax checker establishes that a formula is well formed. A semantic
compiler translates it to a target representation. A certificate checker
validates a conclusion about that representation. If compilation adds
auxiliary variables, the required condition is preservation under projection
onto the original variables, not literal identity of the enlarged assignment
sets.

v satisfies source_formula
  iff there exists auxiliary assignment a:
      (v,a) satisfies compiled_constraints.

Official SMT-LIB supplies a useful precedent for declaring sorts, signatures,
logics, and solver results, including unknown. It is not a license to combine
arbitrary theories without a sound backend [R4]. Spatial position is
intentionally absent from these interfaces. Repainting or rotating the array
must leave every semantic answer unchanged.

A THREE-DIMENSIONAL ATLAS AROUND THE CORE
==============================================================================

The global coordinate convention
--------------------------------

A record receives a display location, but its logical role remains an explicit
tag. A core cube is centered at the origin. Other cubes form layers around it,
with corridors for interaction links. The diagram is an axonometric view of a
sparse cluster, not a solid block of independently valued voxels.

Figure 1. Axonometric atlas of a central shared-theory cube and six role
cubes. North/+x holds diagnostics; south/-x holds cases; +y holds provenance;
-y holds witnesses; +z and -z show Q and NOT Q regions. Dashed spokes are
coordinate guides, not implication arrows. Logical interactions use separately
typed ports.

Region        | Assigned function                                      
--------------+--------------------------------------------------------
Origin        | Shared assumptions and certified common consequences   
x < 0, south  | Cases, inputs, local premises, concrete witnesses      
x > 0, north  | Coverage checks, conflicts, counterexamples, verdicts  
y < 0         | Witness construction, choice and existence dependencies
y > 0         | Provenance, scope, certificate and revision records    
z > 0 / z < 0 | The positive / negative regions of a named query Q     

Layout is a representation function
-----------------------------------

Let K be the finite set of display records. Choose an injective slot
assignment L:K->Z^3 x Slots, where Slots distinguishes entries inside a cube.
Injection prevents accidental aliasing of unrelated records. Several panels
may deliberately display one record by storing references to its single ID;
such visual aliases are not additional members of K.

The x and y sign conventions are inherited from the project, not standard
Cartesian semantics. Negative y means the witness wing, not the negation of
choice. North means the diagnostic side, not increasing truth. Shared claims
are recorded centrally even when their supporting proof dependencies lie in a
side wing [P1, section 1].

A renderer may rotate the camera or change the visual spacing. It must keep
the role tags, context IDs, and signed-query legend visible. A cube
intersected by several logical relations is connected by ports; touching faces
are not themselves evidence of implication. The complete record graph remains
available in a planar or textual view so occlusion cannot hide a constraint.

This atlas is the general 3D construction. Its mathematical correctness comes
from the semantic layer and the projection contracts established on later
pages, not from a privileged physical shape.

WHAT A CUBE CONTAINS: CELLS, FACES, AND PORTS
==============================================================================

A cube is a typed panel container
---------------------------------

Each displayed cube carries a context and a role. Its faces may expose
different views of the same underlying relation store. One face can show
proposition truth, another implication, another incompatibility, and another
transition information. A face title declares the relation; a global array of
untyped ticks and crosses is not sufficient.

Cube = (cube_id, context_id, role, signature_view, revision).
Cell = (relation_id, ordered_arguments, context_id, formula_id).
Port = (relation_id, endpoint_id, direction, transport_contract).

An implication cell for P -> Q is different from one for Q -> P. An
incompatibility cell is symmetric only if its declared definition is
symmetric. The staircase representation can omit transposed duplicates of a
symmetric relation, but not reverse directions of an asymmetric relation.
Missing display blocks must be reconstructible from a named rule, not guessed
from their geometric symmetry.

Complete storage is a graph or hypergraph
-----------------------------------------

A relation involving several propositions owns a hyperedge connecting every
argument. For a constraint C(P,Q,R), its three ordered argument IDs remain
attached even when the visualization distributes its information across
several pairwise faces. Changing R invalidates the constraint's cached status
and every dependent deduction.

For a concrete geometric realization, choose cube half-side h and spacing s
with s greater than twice h. Integer triples locate disjoint cube interiors:

Cube(i,j,k) = [s*i-h,s*i+h] x [s*j-h,s*j+h] x [s*k-h,s*k+h].

The origin is reserved for the core. Enumerate remaining slots by successive
finite shells max(|i|,|j|,|k|)=r. Reserve dominant negative-x slots for cases,
positive-x for diagnostics, negative-y for witnesses, and positive-y for
provenance; a fixed priority handles ties. Within case slots, k's sign follows
the active query. Local face coordinates carry row and column positions. This
supplies an injective allocator for any finite collection of records without
equating memory addresses with truth.

Packing and the earlier binary array
------------------------------------

Binary row/column addresses remain a possible storage technique, but they do
not define the logic. A record ID may be packed into coordinate fields or
mapped to sparse pages. Unoccupied capacity, a known-false proposition, an
unsupported proposition, and a deleted revision require different tags. Zero
padding must never be interpreted as a collection of logical negations.

A routed connector carries its endpoint IDs and relation type. Allocate a
distinct routing lane when lines cross; geometric contact does not create an
argument or inference. This is a labeled graph display, not a theorem that
every relation admits a prescribed nearest-neighbor cubic embedding. Moving a
cube preserves its identity and dependencies.

These rules establish completeness of the display interface: all semantically
required data remain in the backing structure. They do not establish
completeness of inference. The latter depends on the solver contract and the
joint constraints discussed on pages 11 and 18 [R1].

PAGE 07 / 39 | AXES AND INTERACTIONS
THE X AXIS: CASES TO A DIAGNOSTIC PYRAMID
==============================================================================

Southern case blocks
--------------------

The negative-x side stores alternative interpretations, local assumptions, and
observations. A case block represents a set of models, not necessarily a
single fully specified model. Overlapping case blocks are permitted during
input collection, provided their memberships are retained. Orthogonalization
later replaces their overlap by explicitly labeled intersections.

The southern store must distinguish a premise offered for testing from a
premise admitted as a hard fact. A manually selected example is a witness that
one case is possible. Selecting it does not establish that all competing cases
are impossible. The predecessor's nonmutating witness selection is retained as
an invariant [P1, sections 4 and 6].

Northern diagnostic levels
--------------------------

The pyramid has a semantic dependency order. Its base checks syntax, types,
context identity, and premise consistency. Its next level checks coverage,
exact compilation, and survival of alternative cases. The next level stores
proof certificates and countermodels. The apex presents the strongest
justified answer with the query, evidence, logic, and scope still attached.

Admitted premises -> exact encoding -> coverage/search result
                  -> checked certificate -> scoped conclusion.

The arrow is a dependency, not an evidentiary vote. Several paths quoting one
original premise remain several uses of the same premise. A numerical
confidence level cannot repair a missing logical implication. Proof
dependencies must have well-founded justifications even when the represented
system has temporal cycles.

Proof levels versus fixed-point iterations
------------------------------------------

For an acyclic derivation record, assign level zero to admitted leaves and
level one plus the largest parent level to a derived record. This gives a
layout rank for the pyramid. It does not measure mathematical depth or truth
strength. A shorter derivation can depend on a stronger assumption than a
longer one.

A temporal fixed-point computation legitimately revisits a state. Its
certificate is not a circular argument that a property holds because it
already holds. The checker verifies a fixed-point equation, a finite
convergence trace, or the appropriate induction/coinduction conditions. That
certificate is then a leaf of the displayed proof dependency graph at a
declared checking boundary.

Example of a correct apex
-------------------------

For E = NOT C in the CH specialization, beta and gamma survive. One satisfies
H and the other NOT H. The apex reports UNDETERMINED and displays that
opposing pair. It does not describe the negative case as a defect to be pushed
out of the grid. For E = C, only alpha survives and H follows in the
current-world scope. A modal query BOX H is evaluated separately and can still
be false.

The positive-x direction therefore identifies the source of confusion:
incompatible premises, missing information, a scope error, an invalid
converse, a lost interaction, or an unresolved computation. It is a structured
diagnostic output rather than an axis of increasingly preferred conclusions.

THE Y AXIS: WITNESSES AND PROVENANCE
==============================================================================

Negative y: existence needs a witness contract
----------------------------------------------

The witness wing records an existential claim, its domain, the conditions
under which it is required, and a candidate or symbolic witness. Verification
evaluates the original predicate at the witness. A family of local witnesses
must retain the indices on which the selections depend.

For every context w, there exists t with Phi(w,t).
There exists t such that, for every w, Phi(w,t).
There exists f such that, for every w, Phi(w,f(w)).

These formulas have different meanings. The first does not imply the second.
Under the appropriate set-indexed nonempty-fiber hypotheses, AC yields the
third; it need not supply a computable f. In a finite table with all allowed
values explicitly available, a fixed enumeration provides a selection without
invoking full AC. The forcing-specific role of choice is developed on page 23
[R8].

For a two-context example, let the permitted witness be a token named left in
one context and right in the other. The case-dependent assignment works
throughout the family, but neither constant token does. The center may contain
the existential statement while the different witnesses occupy southern and
negative-y records.

Positive y: a fact is not its evidence source
---------------------------------------------

The provenance wing stores the origin of an assertion, the rule used to
transform it, its revision, and its precise scope. A derived entry references
its parents. Imported theorems carry hypotheses and source locators; synthetic
examples are labeled synthetic; externally reported observations carry
observation IDs rather than becoming unconditional axioms by display
placement.

The mathematical assertion AC@w and the metatheoretic record that a proof uses
AC are distinct. The former concerns a represented world. The latter concerns
the justification of an inference in the reasoning environment. A shared
notation must not merge these levels [P1, section 2].

Interactions across the y axis
------------------------------

A witness certificate can justify an existential cell in a case block. A
provenance check can invalidate that certificate if its domain or revision
changed. That invalidation does not assert that the existential proposition is
false; it withdraws the specific support until another justification is
available.

The exact engine therefore separates theorem status from computational or
evidential status. A formula may be mathematically entailed by a model while
its available proof record has not yet been checked. A source can assert P and
another assert NOT P without either becoming a hard fact. The
inconsistent-information adapter keeps both reports visible on page 28.

No multiplication of probability weights is licensed merely because two proof
paths exist. Repeated references to the same source observation remain one
source event. A dependency ledger preserves logical reuse while preventing
that reuse from being misrepresented as independent evidence.

THE Z AXIS: SIGNED TRUTH REGIONS AND AMBIGUITY
==============================================================================

A signed chart is relative to a named query
-------------------------------------------

Fix a classical query Q and a nonempty semantic domain Omega. Define the
positive and negative regions by its truth value. These are not additional
dimensions of the source logic; they are an explicit partition used by the
display.

Omega_plus  = [[Q]].
Omega_minus = Omega minus [[Q]].
Omega_plus INTERSECT Omega_minus = EMPTY.
Omega_plus UNION Omega_minus = Omega.

The notation is relative to the current context, theory, and model semantics.
Q may be CH in the inherited example or ActuatorEnabled in an engineering
instance. Relabeling the query changes the signed chart; it does not change
the underlying model or turn an old proof into a proof about a different
proposition.

The central plane is an evidence view
-------------------------------------

For evidence region E, compute E_plus = E intersect [[Q]] and E_minus = E
minus [[Q]]. If both are nonempty, the evidence is ambiguous about Q. That
ambiguity is a union spanning the two sides, not a third classical world where
Q is partly true. It also differs from inconsistent support for both Q and NOT
Q.

A single classical world cannot be placed on both sides of its own Q chart.
Distinct worlds may occupy opposite sides, including worlds connected by an
allowed transition. Thus logical disjointness is not temporal inaccessibility.
A transition from a Q-world to a NOT-Q-world does not produce Q AND NOT Q at
either endpoint.

Several questions need several charts
-------------------------------------

One vertical sign cannot simultaneously encode every proposition's truth value
unless a full signature is stored behind it. The general representation uses a
partition by a finite ordered list of queries, with one sign component per
query. A chosen chart displays one component or a named grouping; all other
components remain in the case signature.

For example, H and C in the CH specialization yield alpha, beta, gamma, not
merely two undifferentiated halves. Alpha and beta are positive in the H chart
but lie on opposite sides in the C chart. The shared identities permit
changing the view without recomputing which complete cases are valid.

Signed projections
------------------

Pi_plus(E)  = E intersect [[Q]].
Pi_minus(E) = E intersect [[NOT Q]].
Pi_plus(Pi_minus(E)) = EMPTY.
Pi_plus(E) UNION Pi_minus(E) = E.

The identities follow from complementarity. Their usefulness is exact recovery
of the evidence region from disjoint pieces. The physical height assigned to a
positive cube has no effect on any equation. In nonclassical adapters,
complementarity itself must be reconsidered; a signed classical chart cannot
be imported unchanged into every logic.

THE CENTRAL BOX: A SHARED THEORY, NOT A FAVORED WORLD
==============================================================================

Membership in the center
------------------------

For a nonempty model family W, the semantic center is the intersection of its
theories. An implementation stores a finite set of checked center claims, not
an uncomputed complete theory of arbitrary structures.

Core(W) = {phi : every w in W satisfies phi}.
StoredCore = a finite certified subset of Core(W).

A shared input assumption belongs to a declared theory contract. A derived
common fact has a separate certificate relative to that contract. The display
must label the distinction. Neither position in the center nor agreement among
examples upgrades an empirical assumption into an unconditional theorem.

Two opposite monotonicities
---------------------------

If a larger family of contexts is admitted, fewer statements may be common to
all of them. If evidence narrows the candidates, more statements may follow
conditionally.

W subset W' -> Core(W') subset Core(W).
EMPTY != E' subset E -> Core(E) subset Core(E').

Proof. A universal statement over a larger set is true on every subset. Apply
that observation first to W subset W' and then to E' subset E. The directions
differ because the first operation adds possibilities, whereas the second
removes them.

The empty case is deliberately excluded from the second assertion as an
interface rule. Classical universal quantification over an empty set is
vacuous, but a reasoning tool should display inconsistent premises rather than
promote all formulas to substantive common truths.

CH and choice in the center
---------------------------

In the retained family, AC, C -> H, and BOX AC are shared. H is not shared
because gamma refutes it. NOT H is not shared because alpha and beta refute
it. Evidence C makes H conditionally established; it does not move H into the
original global center [P1, sections 2-3].

The center can also contain a statement of the form EXISTS t Phi(t) with
case-dependent witnesses, provided the quantifier semantics and scope justify
it. A shared existence claim need not have a single constant witness across
the family. This is why witness records remain linked rather than merged by
name.

A fixed-core admission rule
---------------------------

A new context entering a model family with a fixed shared contract must
satisfy that contract. If its admission is only hypothesized, the family
becomes conditional on that hypothesis. If an actual new counterexample
violates an old derived center claim, either the family has changed, a premise
failed, or an earlier proof was unsound. The history should identify which
event occurred.

Thus the middle box is the stable interface for comparison, not a collection
of claims selected because they look central to the discussion. Its nonempty
scope and dependency certificates are what make its contents common.

INTERACTIONS THAT PAIRWISE FACES CANNOT RECOVER
==============================================================================

A complete staircase can still be logically incomplete
------------------------------------------------------

Suppose P, Q, R are Boolean propositions and the retained relation is R <-> (P
XOR Q). Every assignment to a selected pair extends to a satisfying value of
the third proposition. Therefore all three pairwise compatibility tables are
unrestricted. Yet the complete relation excludes the joint assignment P, Q, R
all true.

Allowed full profiles:
  NOT P AND NOT Q AND NOT R
  NOT P AND Q AND R
  P AND NOT Q AND R
  P AND Q AND NOT R

Pairwise projection has erased the essential interaction. Adding every mirror
image of every pairwise block cannot restore it. This example is checked
exhaustively by the companion verifier.

The exact global object is a join of constraints
------------------------------------------------

For variables with domains D_i, let each constraint relation F_j have a
declared scope S_j. A global assignment is valid exactly when every scoped
restriction belongs to its corresponding relation.

Omega = {v in PRODUCT_i D_i :
         for every j, restriction(v,S_j) belongs to F_j}.

The relation store retains S_j and every allowed tuple or an equivalent exact
formula. Pairwise faces are views of that information. They are sufficient
only when a lossless-decomposition theorem has been established for the
particular constraint family.

A Boolean exclusive-or constraint can be represented by a labeled factor cube
whose ports connect P, Q, and R. No cell is treated as an independent
proposition merely because it lies at a different coordinate. The three-way
factor is evaluated as a whole, and a failed tuple propagates back to the
corresponding alternative cases.

Local propagation and global impossibility
------------------------------------------

There is a second obstruction even when the original constraints themselves
are pairwise. Require P != Q, Q != R, and P != R over Boolean values. Each
value of each variable has support across every individual relation.
Nevertheless no complete assignment exists: two Boolean values cannot be
assigned pairwise distinctly to three variables.

Local consistency routines can eliminate impossible values efficiently, but
local support alone is not a global satisfiability certificate. The classic
constraint-network framework explicitly combines consistency propagation with
case analysis [R1].

Construction rule
-----------------

The array is complete when every source constraint retains all of its
arguments, semantic rules, and provenance; every displayed summary has an
exact interpretation; and the global checker accounts for all constraints
jointly. It is not complete merely because every pair of proposition labels
has a visible cell.

This distinction is especially important for quantifier dependencies, modality
nesting, causal conditions, and exception rules. Their interaction scopes can
exceed any one face. The physical cube must therefore serve a
hypergraph-backed interface rather than dictate the arity of the logic.

PAGE 12 / 39 | BOOLEAN CORE
EXACT TRUTH STATUS AND THE TWO-QUERY TEST
==============================================================================

A formula and an evidence state are different objects
-----------------------------------------------------

Let Omega be a nonempty set of models of Gamma. Evidence selects E subset
Omega, while a query selects [[Q]] subset Omega. Compute the positive and
negative survivor regions independently. This avoids inferring necessity from
one successful witness.

Positive survivors | Negative survivors | Result      
-------------------+--------------------+-------------
Present            | Absent             | ESTABLISHED 
Absent             | Present            | EXCLUDED    
Present            | Present            | UNDETERMINED
Absent             | Absent             | INCONSISTENT

These are metalevel statuses of a classical query, not four truth values of Q
at one classical world. A fifth operational result, UNKNOWN, is needed when an
incomplete or budget-limited backend cannot establish which row applies.

SAT interpretation
------------------

For classical propositional premises and evidence, ask whether each of the
following has a solution:

Gamma AND Evidence AND Q.
Gamma AND Evidence AND NOT Q.

If the first is satisfiable and the second is certified unsatisfiable, Q
follows. Reversing the outcomes excludes Q. Satisfying assignments to both
give an explicit ambiguity pair. If both are certified unsatisfiable, the
premises are inconsistent. A solver returning unknown must not be silently
counted as unsatisfiable [R4].

The truth-extraction theorem
----------------------------

Assume the two searches are semantically exact and their certificates pass.
Every model of Gamma AND Evidence either satisfies Q or its classical
negation. The two search domains therefore partition all retained models. The
four combinations above exhaust the possibilities and have precisely the
displayed meanings. This proves soundness and completeness of the finite
verdict procedure, conditional on complete search.

A smaller test can establish an entailment by checking Gamma AND Evidence AND
NOT Q alone, but a satisfiability check of the premises is still required to
distinguish a substantive conclusion from vacuous entailment under
contradiction.

Useful output records
---------------------

An established result includes its premise scope and exclusion certificate. An
undetermined result includes models from opposite sides or another exact proof
of their existence. An inconsistent result includes a contradictory subset
when available. A subset-minimal contradictory set means no member can be
removed while preserving contradiction; it need not have smallest cardinality
among all contradictory subsets.

An excluded formula is false throughout the evidence scope. A formula with one
counterexample is merely not established throughout that scope; it can still
be true at other retained worlds. Keeping these meanings separate prevents the
northern pyramid from reducing all failures to a generic cross mark.

THE ATOMIZATION THEOREM
==============================================================================

From atomic propositions to atomic cases
----------------------------------------

Fix a nonempty semantic domain Omega and interpreted generators P_1,...,P_m.
These generators may be primitive propositions or previously checked formulas.
For each truth signature sigma, form the region where every generator has the
specified truth value.

a_sigma = INTERSECTION_i L_i,
where L_i = [[P_i]] if sigma_i is true,
      L_i = Omega minus [[P_i]] otherwise.
A = {a_sigma : a_sigma is nonempty}.

Theorem O1. A is a disjoint exhaustive partition of Omega. Every generator is
a union of cells in A. Every Boolean combination of those generators is also a
union of cells in A. These nonempty cells are the atoms of the generated
finite Boolean algebra.

Proof of coverage and orthogonality
-----------------------------------

Every w in Omega gives exactly one signature by evaluating the generators, so
w belongs to its signature region. This proves coverage. If two signatures
differ, some generator is required true by one and false by the other. Their
regions have empty intersection. Hence the surviving regions are pairwise
disjoint.

For a fixed generator P_i, every point of [[P_i]] has a signature with sigma_i
true, and every such signature region lies inside [[P_i]]. Their union is
exactly [[P_i]]. Boolean operations act on these unions by intersection,
union, and complement, so structural induction proves reconstruction for every
formula generated from them.

Finally, every Boolean combination has constant truth value on a signature
region. No nonempty proper subset of that region belongs to the generated
algebra. Thus each surviving region is an atom. The theorem is relative to the
observed generators: several detailed worlds can lie in one atom if the
language does not distinguish them.

Computational condition
-----------------------

For an explicitly enumerated finite Omega, the theorem gives a terminating
algorithm. For an infinite model family, the regions still define a finite
observable partition, but deciding whether an individual region is nonempty
may be difficult or undecidable. An implementation must retain an unresolved
region until its nonemptiness or emptiness is justified; the display cannot
certify an atom by appearance alone.

In a finite propositional problem whose generators are all primitive
variables, each surviving signature is a complete satisfying assignment. In a
modal model whose generators are only current atomic labels, the cells need
not preserve future behavior. Page 21 supplies the additional quotient
condition.

Meaning of orthogonalization
----------------------------

Orthogonalization is therefore a refinement of overlapping proposition regions
into mutually exclusive cases, with reconstruction maps retained. It is not a
permutation of the original proposition names, an assertion of statistical
independence, or a physical rotation of their display locations.

CONSTRUCTIVE ORTHOGONALIZATION AND RECONSTRUCTION
==============================================================================

A splitting procedure with an invariant
---------------------------------------

Start with the partition containing Omega alone. Process the chosen generators
in a declared order. Replace every current block b by its two intersections
with the next generator and its complement, omitting only children proved
empty.

A_start = {Omega}.
Split_Q(b) = {b INTERSECT [[Q]], b minus [[Q]]} minus {EMPTY}.
A_next = UNION_{b in A_current} Split_Q(b).

Theorem O2. After each completed step, the retained blocks are disjoint, cover
Omega, and reconstruct every generator processed so far. Proof. The two
children of a parent are disjoint and their union is the parent. Children of
distinct parents cannot overlap because they are subsets of disjoint parents.
Previously reconstructed regions are rebuilt by replacing each parent by its
children. The new generator is the union of all positive children.

Figure 2. One parent region a splits into the positive child a AND Q and the
negative child a AND NOT Q. The children are disjoint and reconstruct the
parent by union. Descendants of distinct parents remain disjoint.

The algorithm ends after finitely many generators, producing the partition of
O1. Its output can be exponentially large, so an actual implementation may
store it symbolically rather than materialize every block.

Preserve overlap signatures
---------------------------

A tempting shortcut takes overlapping regions A, B, C and replaces them by A,
B minus A, and C minus (A union B). This is disjoint, but the resulting pieces
do not preserve all original membership information unless the removed
overlaps are recorded separately. A point in A might or might not belong to B,
and a single block A no longer answers that question.

Complete signature splitting preserves those distinctions. It includes regions
such as A intersect B, A minus B, and the parts outside both when nonempty. A
priority-based first-match partition is appropriate only when the task
explicitly asks for first-match behavior rather than all logical interactions.

Parent maps and certificates
----------------------------

Each child stores its parent ID, its splitting formula, its branch sign, and
the certificate for its nonempty or unresolved status. Reconstruction
references children by identity, not by display position. Eliminating a branch
requires an emptiness certificate under the current hard theory; a low
heuristic score is insufficient.

When the theory itself is strengthened, reconstruction is relative to the
updated allowed universe. A parent may then have no surviving child. That is a
new exclusion, not a pure representation change. Recording the theory revision
alongside the parent map prevents the two operations from being confused.

The complete pipeline is therefore split, validate, retain, record ancestry,
reconstruct. Reordering occurs after these semantic operations and cannot
replace them.

ORDERING THE ATOMS WITHOUT CHANGING THE LOGIC
==============================================================================

Canonical content and chosen order
----------------------------------

The set of nonempty signature regions is independent of the order in which the
same generators are processed. Each region is defined by the conjunction of
one literal per generator; conjunction commutes. Its displayed signature
changes order, but its denotation does not.

A deterministic atlas order first groups atoms by the active signed query,
then by stable parent, then by their new branch signature. A separate physical
layout may prioritize closely interacting constraints. The registry keeps both
orders explicitly, rather than making a visual coordinate serve as the sole
identity.

Semantic ID = (context revision, normalized signature).
Display key = (role, signed query, parent ID, local rank).
Permutation changes the display key, not the denotation.

The permutation obstruction
---------------------------

Let M_ij record whether proposition regions P_i and P_j overlap. Reordering
rows and columns by a permutation gives the same intersections with different
indices. A nonempty off-diagonal intersection cannot become empty merely by
moving its labels. Simultaneous permutation also cannot turn an asymmetric
accessibility relation into a symmetric one.

Thus a final diagonal intersection matrix requires actual disjoint case
regions, not an aesthetically successful arrangement of the original
propositions. Overlapping predicates are recovered as unions of those regions.
A shared proposition whose support is the entire universe belongs in the core
rather than being counted as an additional disjoint atom.

A semantic order and a computational order
------------------------------------------

A fixed variable order supports reproducible reduced ordered binary decision
diagrams. A good order can make a particular function compact; a poor order
can make the representation much larger. No one order is established here as
optimal for arbitrary formulas [R2]. Dependency-based heuristics may place
related arguments near one another, but their output is an optimization
proposal, not a theorem about logical independence.

A block may therefore have a human-readable order and a backend order,
connected by a checked permutation map. Reordering a compiled object must
preserve every truth region and every inverse reference. A regression test
evaluates the same query before and after the transformation and checks exact
equality; the general justification is the explicit bijection of indices.

Extension without renumbering history
-------------------------------------

New descendants receive persistent IDs below their parent. An old proof
continues to refer to its old theory revision and old atom family, while a
transport map identifies its surviving refinement in the new revision. If the
extension changes the meaning of a predicate or the interpretation of a
modality, a mere index map is insufficient. That is a semantic change
requiring a new certificate.

The final order is therefore a stable presentation of an already proved
partition. It makes orthogonality inspectable without suggesting that a
sequence of rearrangements can discover truth absent from the constraints.

BOOLEAN PROJECTORS AND THE LIMIT OF PHYSICAL 3D
==============================================================================

The projection algebra
----------------------

For an event A subset Omega, define Pi_A(E)=E intersect A. Direct substitution
proves idempotence and composition:

Pi_A o Pi_A = Pi_A.
Pi_A o Pi_B = Pi_(A INTERSECT B) = Pi_B o Pi_A.
A INTERSECT B = EMPTY -> Pi_A o Pi_B is the zero map.

For an atomic partition a_1,...,a_k, the projections are mutually annihilating
and their disjoint union reconstructs any E. This is the exact Boolean meaning
of orthogonal components in the array. All of these event projections commute;
no quantum-mechanical measurement rule is being introduced.

An optional vector realization
------------------------------

Assume Omega is finite. Represent each proposition A by its indicator vector
over Omega. Then the pointwise product of two indicators is the indicator of
their intersection. With positive weights w_v, their weighted inner product is

<A,B> = SUM_{v in Omega} w_v * 1_A(v) * 1_B(v).

Because every weight is positive, this equals zero exactly when A and B are
disjoint. With merely nonnegative weights, zero inner product implies only
that their intersection has weight zero; it need not be logically empty. That
qualification matters for continuous probability models and zero-probability
possibilities.

Another realization uses one formal basis vector for each atom. Every
proposition is encoded by its atom-membership coefficients. The resulting
semantic vector space has dimension k, even if its records are displayed in
3D. The scalar product is an optional representation of the Boolean structure,
not its origin.

Why every atom cannot be a perpendicular physical axis
------------------------------------------------------

Theorem O3. A family of nonzero pairwise orthogonal vectors in Euclidean
three-space contains at most three members. Proof. In any linear relation
among them, take the inner product with a chosen vector. Orthogonality
eliminates every other coefficient, and its positive squared length forces its
own coefficient to vanish. Hence the vectors are linearly independent;
three-space has dimension three.

A 3D projection of a higher-dimensional semantic basis may overlap vectors or
alter angles. It remains a display, not an isometry. Similarly, Gram-Schmidt
applied to overlapping proposition indicators can create negative or
non-Boolean coordinates. It supplies an orthogonal vector basis but not
generally a partition into meaningful logical cases.

The construction therefore has two compatible layers: a sparse
three-dimensional atlas for navigation and a Boolean or otherwise typed
semantic algebra for reasoning. Their relationship is explicit encoding and
decoding, not an assertion that all logical orthogonality is literally spatial
perpendicularity.

COMPRESSION WITHOUT LOSING THE COMPLETE CASES
==============================================================================

Full semantics does not require a full truth table
--------------------------------------------------

The atomization theorem describes all relevant cases. It does not require
storing a separate row for every case. A symbolic Boolean function can
represent their union, while a query computes intersections with that
function. This is the practical role of decision diagrams and satisfiability
encodings.

For a Boolean formula F and a chosen variable P, Shannon decomposition gives

F = (P AND F[P:=TRUE]) OR (NOT P AND F[P:=FALSE]).

The two branches are disjoint. A binary decision diagram stores such branches,
shares identical residual subfunctions, and removes a test when both outcomes
lead to the same residual. Under a fixed variable order and the standard
reduction rules, equivalent Boolean functions have the same reduced
representation [R2].

Sharing a residual subgraph is not an assertion that its different path
prefixes are identical. The prefix conditions preserve the context in which
that residual is reached. A terminal TRUE node may stand for many complete
assignments, not one unique atom. The workbench must retain this
interpretation when displaying a collapsed cube.

Definitional SAT compilation
----------------------------

A conjunction node can be represented by a new symbol s together with s <-> (a
AND b). The equivalence is encoded by clauses (NOT s OR a), (NOT s OR b), and
(s OR NOT a OR NOT b). Other Boolean nodes have corresponding exact
definitions. Assert the root symbol to ask for a satisfying source formula.

F(v) is true
  iff there exists an auxiliary assignment a:
      Definitions(v,a) AND Root(a) is true.

The proof evaluates the syntax tree from leaves to root in one direction and
uses the defining equivalences in the other. Merely asserting Root without the
definitions loses the formula. Merely proving equisatisfiability is also
insufficient for every query unless projection back to the original variables
is preserved.

Different backends answer different questions
---------------------------------------------

SAT can produce one satisfying assignment or an unsatisfiability result. A
binary decision diagram can also represent all cases compactly when structure
permits. Exact counting, projected counting, and sampling require separate
algorithms and contracts. Auxiliary assignments must not be counted as
distinct source models without justification.

The 3D atlas should display the semantic region represented by a compiled
object, its unresolved budget, and its certificate interface. It should not
expand a compact representation simply to make every conceptual cube visible.
Conversely, visual collapse is not permission to merge genuinely different
constraints.

This is where the architecture can improve on a manual logic-puzzle grid:
shared residual computations, incremental queries, and preserved evidence maps
can avoid repeating the same deductions across many related cases. No general
polynomial-size representation is promised [R2, R4].

PROPAGATION, SEARCH, AND CERTIFICATE CHECKING
==============================================================================

A sound narrowing invariant
---------------------------

Let C be the exact set of original solutions and D the currently represented
candidates. Every pruning rule must establish that its retained condition is
necessary for C. Then intersection preserves all solutions.

C subset D and C subset E
  -> C subset (D INTERSECT E) subset D.

Proof is direct membership. A solution belongs to both intersected sets and
remains present. Repeating certified intersections preserves the invariant by
induction. This is the same source-level discipline used in the project's
broader solver material [P4].

A propagation routine runs a work queue of constraints affected by a changed
domain or truth entry. Each accepted update records its reason and wakes
dependent relations. On a finite domain, strictly decreasing candidate domains
permit only finitely many deletions. A queue can still cycle through redundant
checks unless its scheduling and cache invalidation are well designed.

Fixed point does not mean solved
--------------------------------

The Boolean disequality triangle on page 11 remains locally supported yet
globally impossible. A propagation fixed point therefore means that the chosen
rules can make no further deduction, not that a global model exists. Complete
finite search branches on an unresolved value and checks both alternatives,
unless another complete backend discharges the remaining obligation [R1].

Branching must preserve coverage. If a region D is split on P, its children D
intersect [[P]] and D minus [[P]] cover D and do not overlap. Closing a child
needs a contradiction certificate. A surviving complete assignment must be
checked against every original constraint, not only against the most recently
active face.

Certificates rather than assertion counts
-----------------------------------------

A SAT witness is checked by evaluating the source premises. An
unsatisfiability certificate may be a resolution derivation whose steps a
smaller checker verifies. A modal model-checking result can be checked by
recomputing the semantic equations over the declared frame. An invariant
certificate needs both initialization and preservation under every allowed
transition.

The proof record stores assumptions used, context, logic adapter, compiler
version, source revision, and checker result. A previously verified proof may
be reused only when its dependencies remain valid. A heuristic changes search
order; it does not authorize deleting a case or marking an unsearched domain
inconsistent.

Result vocabulary
-----------------

The kernel distinguishes a checked witness, checked entailment, checked
contradiction, exact residual representation, and unknown. The distinction is
operationally important: a timeout is not an unsatisfiability proof, and
failure to discover a counterexample is not universal validity. Official
SMT-LIB similarly exposes sat, unsat, and unknown rather than collapsing all
solver outcomes into Boolean truth [R4].

WORKED SYMBOLIC ARRAY: AN INTERLOCK
==============================================================================

Propositions and constraints
----------------------------

Use four named propositions: D = door closed; B = bolt engaged; P = permission
present; E = actuator enabled. These are logical statuses, not numerical
sensor values. Specify the intended interlock by

Gamma = (B -> D) AND (E <-> (B AND P)).

The first rule says that an engaged bolt requires a closed door. The second
specifies exactly when the actuator is enabled. This is a declared engineering
model; actual hardware would need evidence that these rules hold.

The complete orthogonal case array
----------------------------------

Every row below is the conjunction of the displayed literals. The six rows are
the complete satisfying assignments of Gamma, verified by direct enumeration.

Case                               | D | B | P | E
-----------------------------------+---+---+---+--
door-open / no-permit              | F | F | F | F
door-open / permit                 | F | F | T | F
door-closed / unbolted / no-permit | T | F | F | F
door-closed / unbolted / permit    | T | F | T | F
bolted / no-permit                 | T | T | F | F
enabled                            | T | T | T | T

With Q=E, only the enabled case lies on positive z. The others are distinct
negative-side cases, not one merged negative truth assignment. D is
reconstructed by the union of the last four cases, B by the last two, and P by
the rows where its entry is true.

Cross-grid deductions
---------------------

Evidence E eliminates every row except enabled. The checker therefore
establishes B, P, and D. A direct proof provides the same result: E -> B
follows from the biconditional, and B -> D then gives E -> D. That implication
can enter the shared center of the modeled family.

The converse D -> E fails. A closed but unbolted case is an explicit
counterexample. Evidence D alone therefore leaves E undetermined, while
evidence E AND NOT D is inconsistent. The case table, implication grid, and
northern diagnostics all agree because they reference one exact model set.

Witness and provenance wings
----------------------------

A positive observation of E is one evidence event. The three deduced
propositions are consequences of that event plus Gamma, not three independent
observations. The proof record must retain the dependence on both interlock
rules. If a sensor reports NOT D at the same time, the system flags conflict;
it must not quietly redefine door closed or move the observation to another
context.

This example demonstrates the general method without CH or forcing: name the
propositions, retain joint constraints, construct complete cases, factor
shared consequences into the core, and recover each original proposition from
the orthogonal partition. Adding dynamics next requires a transition relation
rather than merely adding more columns to this static table.

MODAL LOGIC: RELATIONS BETWEEN WORLDS
==============================================================================

The model and its truth regions
-------------------------------

A finite multi-modal model is M=(W,(R_a),V), with worlds W, one directed
accessibility relation per modality a, and a valuation of atomic propositions.
Each occurrence P@w is world-scoped. The same atomic symbol may be true at
some worlds and false at others.

[[BOX_a phi]] = {w : every v with w R_a v belongs to [[phi]]}.
[[DIA_a phi]] = {w : some  v with w R_a v belongs to [[phi]]}.

Boolean connectives act on world sets. These equations are the linked-grid
rule: a world/formula face obtains its modal entries from a world/world
relation face and the constituent formula's truth face. The companion checker
compares set-based evaluation with an independently written pointwise
recursion.

No hidden frame assumptions
---------------------------

Reflexivity, transitivity, symmetry, seriality, and accessibility by one step
versus any finite path are distinct declarations. On a dead-end world, BOX phi
is vacuously true and DIA phi is false under the ordinary one-step semantics.
Adding a self-loop changes those values. The predecessor uses a reflexive
reachability relation; another adapter must not inherit that convention
accidentally [P1; R5-R6].

Current-world evidence selects which starting worlds are possible. It does not
normally remove their future successors. Evaluate the formula in the declared
model and then intersect its truth region with the evidence region.
Restricting the model itself is a different update operation, examined on page
25.

What is being verified
----------------------

Model checking asks whether a formula holds in a supplied model or at selected
worlds. Frame validity asks whether it holds for every admissible valuation on
a supplied frame. Logical validity asks whether it holds throughout a
specified class of frames and valuations. These questions require different
searches and certificates.

A single truth matrix is sufficient for model checking, but not for arbitrary
frame validity. Conversely, using every Boolean valuation may be inappropriate
when the application restricts atomic propositions to interpreted sentences
whose truth values satisfy additional theory constraints. This distinction is
essential in the CH example.

Extension to nesting
--------------------

The semantic equations evaluate formulas by their parse trees. A structural
induction proves correctness: atomic cases use V; Boolean steps use their
exact operations; modal steps use the complete accessibility relation. The
induction covers every finite formula, regardless of nesting depth. It is not
a proof that arbitrary formulas about unspecified infinite frames can always
be decided.

The basis can remain finite even while formulas become longer, because each
formula denotes a subset of a fixed finite W. That finite closure is the
computational opportunity. The next page states when several worlds can safely
share a basis block without losing modal distinctions.

MODAL ORTHOGONALITY NEEDS A STABLE QUOTIENT
==============================================================================

Equal current labels are not enough
-----------------------------------

Take worlds s,t,u. Let P be false at s and t and true at u. Suppose s reaches
u while t reaches only itself. Although s and t agree on P, DIA P is true at s
and false at t. Merging them by their present truth values alone gives the
wrong modal answer.

An orthogonal partition of W can therefore be adequate for current Boolean
observations yet inadequate for future-facing formulas. Modal compression
requires a relation-preserving partition. Bisimulation provides the relevant
standard contract [R5].

A finite refinement algorithm
-----------------------------

Start with blocks whose worlds agree on the tracked atomic valuations. For
every world, record its current block and the set of successor blocks under
each relation. Split a block whenever these signatures differ. Repeat until no
block changes.

Signature(w) = (current_block(w),
                {block(v): w R_a v} for each action a).

Every nontrivial round increases the number of blocks. A finite model permits
only finitely many such splits. The stable partition is independent of
temporary block names; it is determined by the refinement relation, while its
chosen presentation order remains arbitrary.

Preservation theorem
--------------------

Let q map each world to its stable block. Define A R'_a B when a world in A
has an R_a successor in B. Atomic labels are constant on A. Stability ensures
that whenever A R'_a B, every world in A has some corresponding successor in
B.

Theorem M1. For every formula of the tracked ordinary multi-modal language,

w satisfies phi  iff  q(w) satisfies phi in the quotient.

Proof. The atomic and Boolean cases are immediate. For DIA, an original
successor gives a quotient successor. Conversely, a quotient successor is
realized from every representative of the source block by stability; apply the
induction hypothesis there. BOX follows by the same argument or classical
duality. Structural induction completes the proof.

Limits of this quotient
-----------------------

The result preserves modal possibility and necessity, not necessarily the
number of successors, their probabilities, individual identities, or
distances. Graded modalities need multiplicity information. Probabilistic
transitions need matching aggregate probabilities. A first-order formula
naming two separate successors can distinguish models that ordinary modal
logic merges.

The finite verifier checks the quotient on every directed three-world frame
and every valuation of one atomic proposition, including frames with dead
ends. That tests the implementation over a declared range. The induction
above, not the number of checks, justifies arbitrary finite formula nesting
under the theorem's hypotheses.

CH AS THE RETAINED SIGNED CASE STUDY

The inherited specialization
----------------------------

Use H=CH, C=V=L, and AC as interpreted set-theoretic sentences. The
predecessor assumes a countable transitive ground model of ZFC+V=L. Operation
A adds one Cohen real, operation B adds omega_2 Cohen reals, and only finite
histories of these operations are permitted. This is a conditional
model-theoretic setting, not a finite universe satisfying ZFC [P1].

Cohen product and iteration equivalences explain why the histories reduce to
small and large forcing types. Preservation of ZFC and the independence of CH
are imported mathematical inputs; the finite checker does not reprove them
[R6-R7].

Finite Boolean kernel: Gamma = AC AND (C -> H).
alpha = AC AND C AND H.
beta  = AC AND NOT C AND H.
gamma = AC AND NOT C AND NOT H.

The cases are nonempty complete profiles for these tracked propositions. Their
disjointness follows syntactically; their set-theoretic realization depends on
the stated forcing construction. AC is shared, alpha and beta are on positive
z, and gamma is on negative z.

The accessibility interaction
-----------------------------

From / to | alpha | beta | gamma
----------+-------+------+------
alpha     | T     | T    | T    
beta      | F     | T    | T    
gamma     | F     | F    | T    

With this reflexive relation, BOX AC holds everywhere. BOX H holds nowhere,
and DIA BOX NOT H holds everywhere. Evidence C establishes H currently but
does not establish BOX H. Evidence NOT C leaves beta and gamma, so CH remains
undetermined.

Why finite digits do not decide CH
----------------------------------

A ground model and its Cohen extension contain the same finite binary strings,
but the extension can contain a new infinite branch through that tree. CH
concerns the model's entire collection of real numbers, not the value of a
single finite bit record. Agreement on every finite level of the condition
tree is therefore compatible with different CH values in the resulting models
[R7].

Homogeneity makes the relevant parameter-free sentences uniform within a
forcing type; it does not automatically compute their values. A new sentence
may distinguish the ground and small-extension types if the original scope did
not already distinguish them. The V=L specialization here deliberately
supplies such a discriminator. A larger vocabulary naming individual generics
would require reconsidering the quotient.

The center stores the conditional forcing premises and the finite consequences
certified from them. It does not store CH merely because it holds in the
original model. The example illustrates the array's purpose: separate shared
laws, opposing cases, current evidence, and future accessibility without
inventing a truth-selection rule.

CHOICE, FORCING NAMES, AND LOCAL WITNESS MIXING
==============================================================================

What full choice asserts
------------------------

For a set-indexed family (X_j) of nonempty sets, AC supplies a function
selecting an element from each X_j. It is an existence assertion. In the
declared ZFC case family it is true in every world, so its proposition belongs
in the shared center. Its use in constructing witnesses is separately recorded
in the negative-y wing [P1].

A finite family with explicit nonempty domains can be handled by an explicit
selection rule. No general effective method for arbitrary set-theoretic choice
follows from arranging those finite examples in cubes.

The forcing maximum principle
-----------------------------

The full forcing maximum principle says that an existential assertion forced
by p has a forcing name witnessing it:

p forces EXISTS t Phi(t)
  iff there exists a forcing name tau with p forces Phi(tau).

Over ZF, the unrestricted principle for forcing posets is equivalent to AC.
The selection of suitable local names and maximal antichains is part of that
proof dependency [R8]. A specific finite selection problem should not be
described as requiring the full principle merely because it resembles one step
of the proof.

Mixing across orthogonal regions
--------------------------------

Suppose e_i are pairwise disjoint Boolean regions with join TRUE, and local
names tau_i have been supplied. The mixing construction gives a name tau
agreeing with tau_i on its corresponding region. If every region establishes
Phi(tau_i), their union establishes Phi(tau). Boolean-valued forcing and its
witness interpretation are developed in the formal CH verification [R7].

The signed CH example has a finite analogue. Let tau use token-positive on
alpha and beta, and token-negative on gamma. A local acceptance relation
validates the appropriate token on every case. No constant token works
everywhere, but the case-dependent rule does. The finite verifier checks this
distinction through an explicit two-element witness relation.

Epistemic limitation
--------------------

Existence of the mixed rule does not imply that an observer can determine
which branch applies. If observations cannot distinguish the positive and
negative case, an executable decision that selects different tokens from the
same observation is impossible. The forcing name is a semantic object whose
interpretation can vary with the generic extension, not a sensor that
discovers the extension.

This separates three entries that must remain linked but distinct: the shared
existential theorem in the center, the branch-indexed local witnesses, and the
evidence or assumptions enabling a particular selection. Confusing them would
turn a witness-existence theorem into an unwarranted truth oracle.

The construction therefore uses AC exactly where its quantifiers apply. It
does not use the negative-y direction to declare a global algorithm, remove
the negative-CH region, or assign a preferred representative to an unresolved
reality.

TEMPORAL LOGIC AND INFINITE BEHAVIOR OF FINITE STATES
==============================================================================

Reachability is not inevitability
---------------------------------

A finite transition graph can have infinitely long executions because states
repeat. A claim that a goal is reachable differs from a claim that every
execution eventually reaches it. This cannot be repaired by labeling one axis
time while leaving the operator semantics unspecified.

Let Wait have a self-loop and an edge to Done, and let Done have a self-loop.
Done is reachable from Wait. Nevertheless an execution can remain at Wait
forever. Thus EF Done holds at Wait, while AF Done does not. A scheduler or
fairness assumption would be additional structure, not information already
contained in the existence of the successful edge.

Finite fixed-point calculations
-------------------------------

Assume a finite total transition graph: every state has a successor. Define
Pre_exists(X) by existence of a successor in X and Pre_all(X) by all
successors lying in X.

EF Q = least X satisfying X = [[Q]] UNION Pre_exists(X).
AF Q = least X satisfying X = [[Q]] UNION Pre_all(X).
AG Q = greatest X satisfying X = [[Q]] INTERSECT Pre_all(X).

The least fixed points are obtained by starting with the empty set and
iterating the displayed monotone operation. The greatest fixed point starts
with all states and iterates downward. Each strict step changes membership of
at least one state, so the finite procedures terminate. Their interpretation
is established by induction on finite reachability ranks and by the absence or
presence of indefinitely surviving counterexample paths [R3].

The totality hypothesis matters. At a dead end, a naive universal predecessor
is vacuously true and can distort an eventuality calculation. A tool must
either require totality, explicitly add terminal self-loops, or specify
finite-trace semantics. These choices are visible inputs, not implementation
details to hide.

The array representation
------------------------

A temporal face stores state/subformula membership. A relation face supplies
the transitions. A fixed-point face records iteration ranks or an invariant
certificate. A counterexample block can store a finite prefix and repeating
loop witnessing failure of an eventuality. The loop is a valid infinite-path
representation, not a circular proof of its own legality.

The companion test compares AF iteration with an independent search for
reachable cycles that avoid the target on every total three-state graph. That
verifies a finite implementation of the fixed-point contract. Fairness,
real-time bounds, stochastic scheduling, and continuous dynamics are outside
that test.

The retained CH formula DIA BOX NOT H expresses the existence of a state after
which NOT H persists. It does not assert that every history eventually enters
that state. Distinguishing these statements is a direct application of the
temporal layer.

KNOWLEDGE, OBSERVATION, AND ACTION-SPECIFIC UPDATES
==============================================================================

More than one relation can occupy the same atlas
------------------------------------------------

For an agent a, an epistemic relation connects worlds indistinguishable to
that agent. An action relation connects states by a permitted operation. A
temporal relation describes succession. Their Boolean matrices may look
similar, but their interpretations and required frame properties differ.

K_a Q at w: every a-indistinguishable world satisfies Q.
DIA_action Q at w: some permitted action successor satisfies Q.

A shared cube face must name which relation is being used. Equality of the
underlying Boolean matrices in one example does not identify the modalities in
every extension. Multi-agent and refinement modal logic supply established
frameworks for such distinct relations [R5].

A concrete ambiguity
--------------------

Consider beta and gamma in the CH example. Both satisfy AC AND NOT C. An
observer whose observation vocabulary contains only those facts cannot
distinguish them. CH is therefore not known under that observation model, even
though each underlying world has a definite classical CH value.

An extra observation capable of separating the cases can refine the epistemic
region. Repeating an already known invariant observation cannot do so.
Formally, if O(w)=O(v), any deterministic computation applied only to O has
the same output at w and v. A reversible recoding of the observation cannot
change that equality.

Evidence selection versus changing the model
--------------------------------------------

Ordinary current-world evidence E selects admissible starting worlds while
leaving the transition structure used to evaluate future formulas unchanged. A
public announcement update is a different operation: it may restrict an
epistemic model to the worlds satisfying the announced formula and restrict
its relations accordingly.

For a one-step action graph, deleting successors can make a box formula true
that was previously false. Thus interpreting a current sensor reading as a
global removal of all nonmatching future states is unsound unless the update
semantics explicitly require that transformation. The predecessor avoided this
error by evaluating the full modal truth region before evidence selection [P1,
section 7].

Adapter obligations
-------------------

A knowledge or action adapter states its relation constraints, update rule,
treatment of failed preconditions, and evaluation point. If a model
transformation is proposed as an abstraction, it needs a preservation theorem
for the intended formula language. Bisimulation, simulations, refinement
relations, and updates preserve different properties; their names cannot be
substituted for one another [R5].

The northern diagnostics should identify whether uncertainty is factual,
observational, temporal, or computational. A formula can be true in the actual
modeled state but unknown to an agent. Another can be false now yet reachable
later. A solver can also fail to settle a well-defined question. These
distinctions require separate cells and scope tags, not additional unlabeled
geometric directions.

QUANTIFIED FORMULAS AND FINITE DOMAIN ENCODINGS
==============================================================================

Atomic predicates acquire arguments
-----------------------------------

To handle first-order structure, replace a bare propositional name by a
predicate with typed terms, such as Connected(device,port). Quantifiers bind
variables rather than introduce independent checkboxes. The parser must record
binder identity so substitutions cannot capture an unrelated variable.

For an explicitly finite nonempty domain D={d_1,...,d_n}, quantifiers have
exact expansions:

FORALL x Phi(x) = AND_{d in D} Phi(d).
EXISTS x Phi(x) = OR_{d in D} Phi(d).

The equivalence follows from the definition of quantification over that
declared finite domain. Equality must receive its intended semantics;
unconstrained equality symbols are not interchangeable with arbitrary Boolean
atoms. Function symbols require values and functional consistency, while
predicate entries become indexed relation tables.

Quantifier order creates an interaction
---------------------------------------

Over the domain {left,right}, define R(x,y) by y=x. Then FORALL x EXISTS y
R(x,y) is true, while EXISTS y FORALL x R(x,y) is false. The witness can
depend on x, and neither single constant works for both elements.

The witness wing must therefore retain a dependency edge from x to the
selected y. Replacing the first formula by the second discards that edge and
changes the problem. A Skolem function records the allowed dependence in a
satisfiability transformation; it is not permission to assume a uniform
constant witness or to reuse a witness across incompatible contexts.

Finite countermodels and finite scope
-------------------------------------

A complete bounded search establishes a result about the chosen domain size
and declared interpretation. It does not automatically establish arbitrary
first-order validity. For example, a strict transitive relation with every
element having a greater successor has no nonempty finite model, but the
natural-number order supplies an infinite model.

FORALL x NOT R(x,x).
FORALL x,y,z ((R(x,y) AND R(y,z)) -> R(x,z)).
FORALL x EXISTS y R(x,y).

Every tested finite bound can fail without the entire theory being
inconsistent. Conversely, a single finite countermodel is enough to refute a
universal validity claim when that model belongs to the declared class.

Spatial construction
--------------------

A predicate table becomes a factor cube with argument ports. A quantified
formula becomes an aggregation cube whose ports include its domain and witness
dependencies. Grounding is a compilation operation with an explicit domain
certificate. The logical orthogonalization can then operate on the resulting
finite Boolean constraints, provided the source-to-grounded equivalence is
recorded.

For unbounded domains, a proof-oriented first-order adapter must replace
exhaustive finite grounding. Completeness of a proof calculus and termination
of a decision procedure are different claims, as the next page makes explicit
[R10, R13].

HIGHER AND NONCLASSICAL LOGIC: THE ADAPTER BOUNDARY
==============================================================================

Expressibility is broader than automatic decision
-------------------------------------------------

A finite syntax tree can encode deeply nested modalities, higher-order
quantifiers, types, and theorem statements. A spatial atlas can display such a
tree and its dependencies. Neither fact supplies an algorithm that always
decides its truth. Unrestricted first-order validity is undecidable; ordinary
finite propositional truth is decidable by exhaustive evaluation [R10].

This distinction is an established limitation of the task, not an unresolved
conjecture being used as a reason to abandon it. The operational response is
to admit complete decision procedures for suitable fragments, proof search for
larger ones, checked finite countermodels, and explicit unknown results when
none closes the case.

Higher-order semantics must be named
------------------------------------

A higher-order formula can quantify over predicates or functions. In full
semantics those variables range over the full appropriate function spaces.
Henkin semantics uses specified collections closed under the relevant language
operations. The completeness properties of these interpretations differ; a
result for one cannot silently be reported as a result for the other [R11].

An LCF-style proof assistant supplies a practical model for extension: complex
tactics propose derivations, while a small logical kernel controls which
theorem objects can be constructed. HOL Light is an established higher-order
example. Its existence supports the architecture of an external proof-checking
backend; it does not mean that the present finite script implements that
system [R12].

Classical partitions are not universal object-logic laws
--------------------------------------------------------

For intuitionistic reasoning, a proposition not established at a world need
not have its negation established there. Consider a world with a possible
extension that establishes P. At the earlier world P can be unestablished
while NOT P is also unestablished. Replacing object-level P OR NOT P by a
classical exhaustive case split changes that logic.

A classical metalevel computer can still encode the finite semantic states of
an intuitionistic model. It must preserve the intuitionistic evaluation rules
rather than substitute Boolean complement for object-level negation. The same
distinction applies to resource-sensitive systems: copying an assumption may
be legal classically and illegal for a consumable resource. A deontic
obligation also need not imply its factual fulfillment. Making every modality
reflexive would wrongly import that implication into systems that do not
validate it.

The extensible contract
-----------------------

Each adapter declares syntax, domains, models, connectives, consequence
relation, permitted structural rules, and certificate checking. A bridge
between two adapters carries its own semantic preservation theorem. Combining
a temporal operator with an epistemic or paraconsistent operator requires a
joint interpretation; taking rules independently from familiar systems is
insufficient.

The workbench can therefore host other complicated logical forms without
claiming one untyped universal solver. Its generality lies in exact interfaces
and visible proof obligations. The Boolean orthogonal core remains applicable
where its complement and distributive laws are actually valid, or as a
faithful metalevel encoding justified by an adapter.

CONFLICTING INFORMATION WITHOUT CLASSICAL EXPLOSION
==============================================================================

Support is different from truth in a complete world
---------------------------------------------------

The classical verdict UNDETERMINED means that valid models survive on both
sides of a query. INCONSISTENT means that no classical model survives the
admitted hard premises. Neither should be confused with an evidence ledger
that contains both a report of P and a report of NOT P.

A four-valued evidence adapter can represent independent support and
opposition. Use the pair (t,f), where t records support for a proposition and
f records support for its negation. The four states are neither,
supported-only, opposed-only, and both. This is a standard Belnap-style
information interpretation [R9].

NOT(t,f) = (f,t).
(t,f) AND (u,g) = (t AND u, f OR g).
(t,f) OR  (u,g) = (t OR u, f AND g).

The AND and OR inside the coordinates are ordinary Boolean operations on
support bits. The outside operations are the explicitly defined object-level
evidence connectives.

A checked nonexplosion example
------------------------------

Let P have support both and Q have support neither. Then P AND NOT P has
positive support, while Q does not. Under consequence defined by preservation
of positive support, the contradiction does not entail every unrelated
proposition. The finite verifier checks this witness, double negation, and the
displayed De Morgan laws.

These operations do not validate every classical inference rule. In
particular, defining an implication as NOT P OR Q does not automatically make
classical modus ponens sound in this evidence semantics. An implication
connective and its inference rules require their own declaration. The array
must not import them from the Boolean core merely because their notation looks
familiar.

Orthogonality at the correct level
----------------------------------

A classical metalevel partition of complete support assignments remains
possible: the states supported-only, opposed-only, both, and neither are
mutually exclusive metadata cases. But object-level P and NOT P are not
disjoint in the both case. The theorem P AND NOT P = FALSE cannot be reused as
a statement of that object logic.

The z chart therefore changes its legend. It may display positive-support and
negative-support channels with an explicit overlap region, or use separate
cubes for the four metadata states. A two-sided classical truth partition is
retained only in a declared classical view.

Context and source discipline
-----------------------------

A source asserting P and another asserting NOT P occupy different provenance
records. P@w and NOT P@v may even be compatible classical facts at different
worlds. Only after source acceptance and scope alignment do they become
contradictory hard premises about one context. The witness and provenance
wings record those transformations.

This adapter provides a principled way to study inconsistent material while
retaining the original conflicting evidence. It does not certify either report
as reality, and it does not reinterpret an inconsistency in classical set
theory as permission to assert CH and NOT CH in one classical world.

MEASURE, PROBABILITY, AND INDEPENDENCE ARE EXTRA STRUCTURE
==============================================================================

Counting follows a declared finite model
----------------------------------------

If Omega is finite and every world is assigned equal probability, the measure
of a region A is |A|/|Omega|. Conditioning on nonempty evidence E gives |A
intersect E|/|E|. These are exact calculations under the uniform model, not a
consequence of placing equally sized cubes around the center.

A different prior assigns masses m_w to worlds. The measure is then the sum of
those masses. Source dependence, likelihoods, and sampling assumptions belong
in a probabilistic adapter; they cannot be inferred from logical disjointness
or from how often a premise appears in a proof record.

Orthogonality is not statistical independence
---------------------------------------------

Disjoint nonempty events A and B satisfy Pr(A intersect B)=0. If both have
positive probability, Pr(A)Pr(B)>0, so they are not independent. Logical atoms
can be perfectly orthogonal while being maximally exclusive as alternatives.

Zero probability is also not always logical impossibility. In an infinite
probability space an individual point may have zero measure while remaining a
legitimate state. A truth checker must use emptiness or a logical certificate
for exclusion, not a small weight or a measure-zero label.

Refinement does not determine child weights
-------------------------------------------

Suppose two old atoms each have mass one half, and the first splits into two
children. Assigning equal mass to all three new atoms gives the old first
parent total mass two thirds, changing the old probability model. A compatible
assignment could instead give the children one quarter each and leave the
second parent at one half.

Required consistency:
  mass(parent) = SUM_{children of parent} mass(child).

The companion verifier checks this finite counterexample and a compatible
refinement. Equal-looking cubes are therefore not a probability specification.
A fixed counting measure at each stage need not be consistent under projection
between stages.

Logical continuation versus probabilistic continuation
------------------------------------------------------

Parent maps preserve logical possibility when every old case has an extension.
Probability preservation additionally requires the mass equation above.
Probabilistic state aggregation likewise needs matching total transition
probabilities into every block; modal reachability preservation alone is
insufficient.

A weighted vector realization on page 16 can connect disjoint regions with
orthogonal indicators. It still depends on positive weights and an explicit
universe. A geometrical metric on display coordinates is independent of that
algebraic inner product. Neither supplies an empirical probability that CH
holds.

These distinctions make the array useful for uncertainty analysis without
turning logical cells into unsupported numerical confidence. A probabilistic
result should retain its prior, observation model, source IDs, and
normalization scope alongside the logical support on which it is computed.

PAGE 30 / 39 | CONTINUATION
GROWING THE LANGUAGE, THEORY, OR WORLD FAMILY
==============================================================================

Three changes that look similar on screen
-----------------------------------------

Adding a new proposition symbol expands the language. Adding a hard constraint
narrows the allowed interpretations. Adding a new world to the comparison
family can enlarge the semantic universe. All can add cubes, but their effects
on truth and the shared center differ.

For nested finite signatures P_n and theories Gamma_n, define C_n as the
satisfying assignments on P_n. When Gamma_(n+1) includes the old theory,
restriction to old variables is a well-defined map

rho_n : C_(n+1) -> C_n.

Its image may be a proper subset of C_n. A new constraint can legitimately
eliminate an old possibility. Purely representational refinement instead
requires the stronger property that every old assignment has an extension.

A finite extension criterion
----------------------------

Let x denote old variables and y new variables. The exact surjectivity
condition is

FORALL x [Gamma_n(x) -> EXISTS y Gamma_(n+1)(x,y)].

On finite Boolean domains this is a quantified Boolean obligation. If it
holds, no old possibility disappears. It also implies that no genuinely new
consequence expressed solely in the old language is introduced: an old
counterexample extends to a new counterexample. Conversely, if some old
valuation fails to extend, the negation of its complete signature becomes a
new old-language consequence.

This proves the equivalence between surjective extension and conservativity
over the old finite propositional language. The result concerns the precise
encoding, not arbitrary new set-theoretic sentences.

A uniform continuation rule
---------------------------

The predecessor's sandbox appends a fresh Q with Q -> H. Assigning Q false
extends every old solution. The same rule works at every finite stage. The
finite verifier checks seven stages, while the explicit extension function
proves the uniform invariant [P1, section 8].

If a new rule instead asserts H in a family previously containing a negative-H
case, that old case has no extension. The change is still a sound theory
strengthening, but it is not a conservative enlargement. A stored parent
record should show that its descendants were excluded by a new premise rather
than merely hidden by layout.

Transporting certificates
-------------------------

A certificate is reusable only through a map preserving its assumptions and
semantics. A proposition-only parent map need not preserve modal operators;
the accessibility conditions of page 21 must also hold. A changed probability
distribution requires the separate mass consistency of page 29. Changing a
word's interpretation is not a variable extension at all.

The source system and its revised version therefore need separate immutable
IDs, an explicit translation, and a record of which preservation property has
actually been established. This is the structural meaning of continuous growth
in the tool.

COHERENT FINITE STAGES AND GENUINE CONTINUITY
==============================================================================

A path is more than a collection of local solutions
---------------------------------------------------

A sequence v_n of finite-stage assignments represents one continued
interpretation only if adjacent assignments agree on the old symbols:

v_n belongs to C_n.
rho_n(v_(n+1)) = v_n for every n.

The collection of such compatible families is the inverse limit of the finite
model sets. Independently choosing a satisfying assignment at each stage need
not produce a compatible family; the choices may disagree on an earlier
proposition.

Finite-to-infinite existence under explicit hypotheses
------------------------------------------------------

Assume finitely many new Boolean symbols at each stage, nested finite
theories, and nonempty satisfying sets at every stage. Compatible partial
assignments form a finitely branching tree with arbitrarily high levels. At a
node with arbitrarily deep descendants, at least one of its finitely many
children also has arbitrarily deep descendants; otherwise all descendants
would have a common finite height bound. Repeating this selection yields an
infinite coherent branch.

Every finitary constraint occurs at some finite stage, so the resulting
interpretation satisfies it. This gives the relevant countable propositional
compactness argument. General logical compactness and its connections with
completeness are established in the classical literature [R13].

The argument takes place in the stated classical metatheory. It does not give
a finite stopping rule for recognizing which child has arbitrarily deep
descendants. If a computable extension function is supplied for every stage,
it does give an effective stream construction. Mere existence of deep
continuations need not supply that function.

Continuity of finite observations
---------------------------------

Give the space of complete Boolean interpretations the topology in which a
basic neighborhood fixes finitely many proposition values. Restricting an
interpretation to an earlier signature preserves such finite observations. An
inverse-limit point is precisely a compatible sequence of these finite
restrictions.

This is topological continuity of information projections, not Euclidean
smoothness in the cube display. A finite observation can be stable while
global facts about an infinite structure differ. The CH condition-tree example
on page 22 demonstrates why shared finite prefixes do not settle the
cardinality of the collection of infinite branches.

Ordinal and infinite-language extensions
----------------------------------------

A set-sized ordinal can index propositions or a directed family of finite
sublanguages. The corresponding compatibility maps must still be supplied. An
ordinal index is not an instruction for a computer to execute infinitely many
stages or to finish a transfinite traversal. Infinite conjunctions, full
second-order semantics, and arbitrary infinite models require separate
theorems; finitary propositional compactness does not cover them
automatically.

Thus the exact continuation claim is conditional and useful: certified finite
projections fit into a coherent global interpretation under stated existence
hypotheses. A finite prefix of successful tests alone is not a certificate of
all future stages.

BOOLEAN IDEALS AND THE MEANING OF A FINITE BASIS
==============================================================================

The algebra at one finite stage
-------------------------------

Let B_n be the Boolean algebra of formulas over the finite signature P_n,
modulo ordinary tautological equivalence. The hard theory Gamma_n determines
the ideal of forbidden regions:

I_n = {[phi] in B_n : Gamma_n entails NOT phi}.

If a region is forbidden, every smaller region is forbidden. The union of two
forbidden regions is forbidden. These facts prove closure under downward
inclusion and finite joins, which are the ideal conditions.

For finite Gamma_n, put F_n = AND Gamma_n. Then the ideal is principal:

I_n = {b in B_n : b <= NOT F_n}.

The proof is Boolean implication: Gamma_n excludes phi exactly when phi
implies NOT F_n. The quotient B_n/I_n identifies formulas that agree on all
satisfying assignments of Gamma_n. Its atoms are the surviving complete cases
from O1.

What is and is not a basis
--------------------------

The surviving cases generate the quotient algebra by unions. They are not
generators of the forbidden-region ideal. The distinction is substantive: the
ideal records exclusions, while the atoms record allowed alternatives. A
layout referring to both as one unspecified finite basis obscures which side
is being represented.

When languages and constraints grow, the old formula algebra maps into the new
one. The induced quotient map is injective exactly when no old distinction is
lost by excluding old cases. Dually, that is the surjectivity of the model
restriction map on page 30. A merely well-defined quotient map can still
collapse an old nonempty region to empty.

Limits can lose atomicity
-------------------------

With infinitely many unconstrained proposition symbols, every nonempty finite
description can be split by a fresh symbol Q. Both a AND Q and a AND NOT Q
remain nonempty. Consequently the finitary Boolean algebra has no minimal
nonempty elements, despite every finite generated subalgebra having atoms.

Similarly, an increasing union of finitely generated exclusion ideals need not
be finitely generated. For example, successively asserting fresh P_i excludes
NOT P_i at every stage. Any finitely many generator formulas mention only
finitely many symbols and cannot express all fresh exclusions. The union ideal
records the entire theory, not one automatically discovered final finite
constraint.

Representation theorem scope
----------------------------

Stone representation gives the established correspondence between Boolean
algebras and suitable spaces. The ordinary general representation uses a
choice principle equivalent to the Boolean prime ideal theorem; choice-free
representations use a different construction [R14]. No such infinite theorem
is required to enumerate one finite truth table.

The justified target is therefore a coherent family of finite orthogonal
partitions and exact quotient maps. A final finite orthogonal basis for every
infinite logical system is not supplied by this construction and is not
required for its finite-stage computational usefulness.

TRUTH ABOUT A MODEL AND TRUTH ABOUT REALITY
==============================================================================

A conditional theorem is not an observation
-------------------------------------------

The array proves consequences of interpreted premises. To connect a
proposition to reality, its observation and interpretation contract must
specify what is measured, when it is measured, which object it concerns, and
which errors remain possible. A syntactically complete theory can still model
the wrong system.

For the interlock example, the theorem E -> D follows from the declared
logical rules. Establishing that an actual device obeys those rules requires a
separate interface argument, testing protocol, or physical specification.
Placing E and D in adjacent cubes cannot supply that evidence.

The observational indistinguishability theorem
----------------------------------------------

Let O be the allowed observation map. Suppose two admissible states w and v
give the same observation but disagree on Q. Any deterministic decoder
receiving only O has the same input in both cases and therefore returns the
same output. It cannot correctly classify Q in both states.

O(w) = O(v),  w satisfies Q,  v satisfies NOT Q
  -> Q is not identifiable from O alone.

The proof is substitution into a function. A reversible change of observation
coordinates preserves equality and cannot repair the ambiguity. Additional
constraints can resolve it only if they are independently justified rather
than selected because they give a preferred conclusion.

Three diagnosis outcomes
------------------------

A countermodel may expose an invalid argument under true premises. A
contradiction may expose jointly incompatible assumptions or observations. An
ambiguity pair may expose missing information. These have different remedies:
revise the inference, investigate the inconsistent inputs, or acquire a
discriminating observation. Rearranging the atoms is useful for showing the
difference, but it is not a replacement for any of those actions.

No statistical band becomes a hard impossibility boundary by drawing it as a
cube face. An uncertain assertion needs an explicit probabilistic or
evidential status. The hard logical solver can then reason conditionally on
that assertion, while the provenance wing records that its admission remains
uncertain.

CH in this boundary framework
-----------------------------

The CH example is mathematical rather than an empirical claim about a sensor.
Its worlds are models of set theory, and its atomic labels stand for
interpreted mathematical sentences. A finite Boolean profile consistent with
AC AND (C -> H) is not by itself a construction of a transitive ZFC model. The
imported forcing theorems justify the selected example's interpretation
[R6-R7].

The practical truth-extraction rule is therefore explicit: identify the model
family and evidence, prove the encoding faithful, check the opposite branch,
and retain the scope of the conclusion. The center contains what is common
under that interpretation. The workbench does not gain access to a
context-free reality merely by adding a positive and negative spatial axis.

EXECUTABLE ARCHITECTURE AND BACKEND INTERFACES
==============================================================================

A layered implementation
------------------------

The implementation blueprint separates the spatial interface from the logical
engine. This prevents a visual edit from silently changing a premise and
permits a nonvisual checker to validate the same exported problem.

Component           | Responsibility                                               
--------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------
Registry            | Stable predicate, context, argument, source, and revision IDs
Parser/type checker | Syntax trees, scope, binders, sorts, admitted operators      
Theory store        | Hard constraints with full interaction scopes                
Semantic adapter    | Interpretation, compilation, query and certificate contract  
Region engine       | Exact case sets or their symbolic representations            
Relation engine     | World transitions and any certified quotient maps            
Proof ledger        | Witnesses, contradiction records, dependencies, checks       
Atlas renderer      | Cubes, faces, ports, signed views, diagnostic pyramid        

The adapter contract
--------------------

An adapter exposes well-formedness checking, semantic translation, candidate
verification, proof verification where available, and a precise result
vocabulary. It declares whether it is a complete decision procedure, a bounded
procedure, a semidecision procedure, or only a certificate checker.
Unsupported formulas are rejected rather than approximately reinterpreted.

Compile : source instance -> target instance + projection contract.
Check   : target claim + certificate -> verified / rejected.
Decode  : target witness -> source witness, under the contract.
Result  : witnessed / entailed / inconsistent / residual / unknown.

For a finite model-checking adapter, Compile preserves the supplied frame and
valuation. For a finite-domain quantified adapter, it records the domain
closure. For a proof-assistant adapter, it records the kernel theory and any
admitted axioms. Evidence updates and model updates are separate transactions.

Transport and invalidation
--------------------------

A source revision change invalidates all dependent claims unless a checked
transport certificate preserves them. Coordinate-only movement changes no
semantic dependency. A new relation edge can invalidate a box formula even
when atomic values remain unchanged. An expanded domain can invalidate a
bounded universal statement. Those events have different invalidation paths
and should not be handled by one generic redraw.

Security of the logical interface
---------------------------------

Imported formulas are parsed as data, never evaluated as arbitrary
host-language programs. The grammar has explicit limits and versioning. An
export records the original premises, compiled representation, decoder, result
certificate, and enough identities to detect aliasing or stale evidence. A
proof checker should not trust a UI badge or an unverified solver exit code.

The companion code implements finite reference mechanisms and replays the
predecessor kernel. This architecture is the specification for a more general
workbench; it is not a claim that all listed adapters have been built.
Established higher-order proof assistants illustrate the small-kernel
approach, including independently verified implementations, without
transferring their certification to this project [R12, R15].

PAGE 35 / 39 | IMPLEMENTATION
COMPLEXITY, MEMORY, AND USEFUL OPTIMIZATION
==============================================================================

Where the work actually resides
-------------------------------

With m independent Boolean generators there can be 2^m nonempty complete
signatures. A full truth table is therefore exponential in m. Atomization
organizes those cases exactly; it does not remove the underlying search in
every problem. Sparse relations and repeated substructure can make particular
instances much smaller.

A Boolean decision diagram can exploit identical residual functions, but its
worst-case size is still exponential and its variable order matters [R2].
Satisfiability, model counting, proof search, and visual layout have different
cost measures. A smaller drawing is not evidence of a smaller semantic
computation.

Fixed-model modal and temporal work
-----------------------------------

For a fixed finite explicit frame, bottom-up modal evaluation inspects atomic
labels and accessibility edges for each subformula. The basic computation
scales with formula size times the represented graph size, before any
additional abstraction costs. Finite temporal algorithms similarly exploit
state-set operations and fixed points; the classical model-checking literature
gives the relevant established complexity results [R3].

Searching over unknown frames is a different task. A finite model-checking
time bound must not be advertised as a bound for arbitrary modal validity,
unbounded quantified satisfiability, or CH truth.

Optimizations with exact interfaces
-----------------------------------

A work queue avoids rechecking unrelated constraints. Shared subformula IDs
reuse truth regions. Bitsets can accelerate finite intersections without
changing their semantics. BDD reordering may reduce memory when accompanied by
equivalence checks. Modal partition refinement can reduce duplicate future
behavior when its preservation contract holds. Every optimization must retain
the original acceptance predicate and reconstruction map.

A split can increase the number of stored blocks while decreasing the allowed
model set. Candidate count and representation size are different. A factor
graph can remain small while its implicit joint model set is enormous. The
interface should report both representation scope and logical coverage rather
than treating either as a runtime estimate.

Practical 3D costs
------------------

The renderer adds its own expenses: picking an occluded cube, routing edges,
labeling contexts, and keeping a stable camera. These costs buy
inspectability, not an asymptotic inference theorem. A synchronized planar
table and searchable proof ledger remain necessary because some dependencies
will be clearer without perspective.

For large instances, display only selected blocks while retaining a visible
statement of what is hidden. Semantic coverage refers to the full backing
model, not to the current viewport. A query that was checked only on selected
visible examples must say so.

The strongest practical advantage over a manual puzzle sheet is reusable exact
state: recorded implications, shared residual computations, explicit
counterexamples, and reliable updates. It is not an assertion that every
difficult high-level logical form becomes easy once it has three display
coordinates.

Compositional correctness
-------------------------

Suppose a source instance is translated to a target representation with exact
forward coverage and backward correctness. Suppose every semantic refinement
preserves all source solutions, every display rewrite preserves IDs, and every
final target certificate is checked. Then a decoded witness satisfies the
original source instance, and a fully covered verified contradiction excludes
all its solutions.

Proof. Forward coverage carries every source solution into a target candidate.
Sound refinements never remove such candidates. Backward correctness maps
every accepted target witness to a source solution. Thus a checked witness is
sound, while exhaustive certified emptiness contradicts the existence of any
source solution. Display permutations contribute no additional premise and
cannot affect this argument.

For modal compression, add the preservation theorem for all required
relations. For probability, add compatible weights. For a nonclassical
language, replace the Boolean laws only through a proved faithful adapter.
These are additional contracts, not consequences of the general composition
statement.

The final form
--------------

The core is a finite certified theory interface. The surrounding blocks retain
every relevant case, including unresolved alternatives and local witnesses.
Hyperedges retain higher-arity interactions. Orthogonalization splits
overlapping regions into disjoint cases and keeps reconstruction. The northern
pyramid reports the outcome of checked searches and proofs with its scope.
Parent maps connect successive finite stages.

This is the requested reusable logic-array construction: three-dimensional in
presentation, algebraically orthogonal where its semantics permit, and
extensible through typed verification interfaces. It can organize arbitrary
finite descriptions of sophisticated logic. Its ability to decide a particular
description remains governed by the chosen language, model class, and backend
guarantee.
