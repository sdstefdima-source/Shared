The rules & the math

White plays against Black. Only rooks, bishops, and knights move. Capture every opposing piece to win. A side with no legal move draws. There are no kings, checks, pawn moves, or automatic repetition draws.

Fixed pawns are neutral, uncapturable terrain. Sliders stop before them. Knights jump over them but cannot land on them.

For each position, the engine ranks material first, then legal mobility, then distinct controlled squares, then defended friendly pieces. Values: rook 5, bishop 3, knight 3. Elimination wins outrank these features.

v = (material, mobility, coverage, defense) G = 29837313·v₁ + 16641·v₂ + 129·v₃ + v₄ V₀(S) = G(S) Vₕ(S) = max Vₕ₋₁(S') [White] Vₕ(S) = min Vₕ₋₁(S') [Black]

All legal moves are considered. Alpha–beta search reuses positions and completes successively deeper searches, up to 10 plies: five turns for each side. A time or node budget can stop it earlier; the completed depth is always shown. Candidate ratings use one common, fully completed depth. An unfinished deeper iteration is discarded.

Colored destinations are relative to the best move found across all White pieces. Green: tied best or close in mobility without a worse material outcome. Amber: a positional trade-off. Red: a worse material outcome or a loss compared with a better alternative. “Enemy reach” marks currently attacked squares; an attacked square can still support a favorable exchange.

Ratings are exact for the displayed horizon and this chosen evaluation, not guarantees beyond it. Search does not extend captures past the horizon. Changing pawn terrain starts a new round.
